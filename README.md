## Projects

* **[My Synthetic Diary](https://eugeniedt.github.io/syntheticdiary/) by Eugenie** - A large language model was trained on diary entries spanning 2017 to May 2026. Every couple of days, the model generates a new entry. The entries are posted without intervention.

* **[Living Archive](https://stephanieneuburg.github.io/living-archive/) by Stephanie** - An experimental, spatial visual-discovery interface for image collections. Not a standard gallery — instead of opening one file at a time in a folder tree, Living Archive arranges a whole folder of images into an interactive atlas: swarms and clusters that surface the relationships between images so you can browse, compare, and notice patterns. [Repository](https://github.com/stephanieneuburg/living-archive)

* **[Rosamondo](https://mtblecha-alt.github.io/rosamondo-study/) by Marie-Therese** - Study for Rosamondo, a B2B marketplace for circular materials — where excess workshop and production materials get a new life.

* **[Ende der Tamponsteuer](https://jama-jpg.github.io/Ende_der_Tamponsteuer/) by Janina** - A website that provides information about the end of the tampon tax in Austria, including details about the tax, its history, and the implications of its removal.

* **[Ready When You Are](https://github.com/boweryelectrix/ready-when-you-are/) by Rebecca**

* **[Portfolio Website](https://j-schaeff.github.io/Portfolio-website-Julia/) by Julia Anna**

* **[ICARUS](https://github.com/thewiki96/ICARUS) by Nicolas Henao** - ICARUS is a browser-based critical game about labor, automation, and the hidden rules of performance under surveillance capitalism.

* **[Draw To Print](https://github.com/danielxhafner/DRAW-TO-PRINT) by Daniel** - A fullscreen drawing utility that converts hand-drawn strokes into vector PDF output and sends it to a printer or archive without any dialogs or confirmation windows.
